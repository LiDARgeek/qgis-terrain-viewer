about=Interactive 3D terrain and surface QC viewer for QGIS. Visualize and compare multiple DEM, DTM, and DSM rasters as either QC points or continuous triangle surfaces in a detached 3D viewport while keeping compact controls docked inside QGIS. Includes adaptive detail, display-only Dense 2x/3x native-Z scatter, high-resolution bilinear imagery RGB sampling with coherent synthetic-point color inheritance, display-only directional relief shading, adaptive screen-space point fill, Z-aware SHP/GeoPackage/GeoJSON line overlays, corridor-based cross-sections, surface-ribbon cross-sections, direct 3D measurement, Z-outlier handling, and cursor-focused navigation. Surface meshes never bridge NoData gaps; measurements and QC statistics remain based on native raster samples. No external Python packages or pip installation are required. Tested with QGIS 3.40.x and QGIS 3.44.x on Windows. QGIS 4 is not yet supported.
